Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

1. Who we are

This policy covers Ekko ("we", "our", "us"): the website at ekko.zone, the dashboard at app.ekko.zone, the Ekko API and the Ekko apps for Android (including the Solana Seeker) and iOS. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@ekko.zone.

2. What we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account and sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Wallet addresses you add. Ekko is watch-only: it reads public blockchain activity for these addresses and never holds private keys. When you connect your phone's wallet, it shares only its public address with Ekko, never its keys.
  • What you set up in Ekko: the reports you subscribe to, the alerts you write, your notification channels and quiet hours.
  • Push notification tokens from your phone, so we can send alerts and new report editions to it.
  • Crash reports from the release apps: the app version, the device model and the screen that crashed. They never include your account, email or wallets.
  • Billing details if you pay for a plan. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number.
  • Usage and security logs: requests to our servers, sign-in attempts and errors, used to run and secure the service.

3. What stays on your phone

  • Face or fingerprint unlock is handled by your phone. Ekko only learns whether the unlock succeeded.
  • The camera is used only when you scan a wallet QR code. Images are read on the device and are not stored or sent.

4. How we use your information

  • To run Ekko: watch the wallets and tokens you chose, write your reports and send your alerts.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To send service messages, such as sign-in codes and changes to your plan.
  • To fix crashes and improve the apps.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Services that process data for us

  • Hosting: our servers run with Hetzner in the European Union.
  • Google Firebase: push notifications to Android and crash reports.
  • Resend: sending email, such as sign-in codes and email alerts.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Envio HyperSync and blockchain nodes: reading public chain history for the wallets and tokens you watch.
  • An AI model provider: turning an alert you write in your own words into a rule Ekko can run. Only the alert text is sent.
  • Channels you connect, such as Telegram, Slack, Discord or your own webhook, receive the alerts you route to them.

We share information otherwise only to comply with the law or to protect Ekko and its users.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can remove wallets, alerts and report subscriptions at any time in the app.
  • You can turn notifications off in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • You can delete your account in the app, under Profile, or on the dashboard. Deleting stops all monitoring and delivery at once and deletes your email, wallets, alerts, reports and devices. We keep billing records where the law requires it, and the history of notifications already sent to you is not yet removed by deletion. If deletion in the app is refused, for example while you have a paid plan or own a team, email support@ekko.zone and we close it for you.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing privacy@ekko.zone. We reply within 30 days.

7. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we tell you in the app or by email.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@ekko.zone. Anything else: support@ekko.zone.
