Why we built Ekko

Built to help teams reach users when onchain change matters

Ekko exists so protocol teams, wallet platforms, and engineers can turn missed protocol moments into timely, governed messages.

Why teams use Ekko

Teams usually know which moments matter. The hard part is identifying affected users or operators, choosing the right reachable channel, and sending a message with enough policy and context to trust.

Ekko reduces that friction so notifications become a reliable operating advantage instead of a manual scramble after the fact.

Who benefits most

  • Protocol teams that need to reach users and operators during treasury, contract, or incident moments.
  • Wallet providers that want to route important customer wallet messages without building a custom notification stack.
  • Teams that need event-grounded messages tied to real delivery channels so users, ops, and support stay aligned.

What we optimize for

We design Ekko to make notifications more useful in day-to-day operations.

Timely user messages

Notifications should reach the affected users and operators before silence creates risk.

Less silent risk

Protocol moments should not disappear into explorers, dashboards, and scripts.

Useful message context

Messages should arrive with enough context that users and teams can move without second-guessing.

Direct support

When teams need help, they should be able to reach a person quickly.
