Common questions

What is Ekko?+

Ekko watches the chain and tells you when something you care about happens, and why it matters. Teams use it to alert their users in their own app and keep a record of every notification. You can set alerts up in plain English in the dashboard, or through an AI agent over MCP.

Which networks does Ekko cover?+

Base and Ethereum are live. Avalanche, Arbitrum and Solana are coming soon. What Ekko can watch varies by network.

Where do alerts get delivered?+

In your app, and by push, email, Slack, Telegram or webhook. Each person has in-app delivery by default and can turn on the others.

Do I have to approve every message?+

No. Once you've set your rules, matching alerts send automatically. Approval is for actions: Ekko can prepare a next step, but only you can carry it out.

Does Ekko control my wallet or funds?+

No. Ekko never holds keys, signs transactions or moves funds. It watches, explains and prepares, and you decide what to do. WalletAlert asks users to sign a message to turn on alerts, which isn't a transaction.

Can my AI agent use Ekko?+

Yes. Point your agent at Ekko's MCP server and it can search, create, update and archive alerts. You choose what it's allowed to do.

What's available today?+

WalletAlert, Alert Studio, the API and Marketplace. Reports and Playbooks are coming soon.

Can I use Ekko as an individual?+

Soon. Individual plans will let you watch your own wallets and positions and hear when something needs your attention. Prices are listed above, and you can contact us to ask for early access.
