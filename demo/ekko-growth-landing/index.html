FAQ

Questions teams ask before installing Ekko.

What is Ekko?

Ekko is wallet-aware growth infrastructure for Web3 apps. It detects wallet behaviour, matches it to custom alerts or playbooks, surfaces actions through WalletAlert, and measures return, conversion, and revenue impact.

What is WalletAlert?

WalletAlert is the user-facing alert surface for connected wallets. It can appear as a dropdown, tab, inbox, notification centre, or mobile surface inside your app.

Can we create custom alerts?

Yes. Teams can create custom alerts based on wallet events, token balances, contract activity, segments, time windows, lifecycle behaviour, risk conditions, and protocol-specific rules.

Does Ekko custody user funds?

No. Ekko is designed as read-only wallet intelligence and alert infrastructure. It watches signals, triggers actions, and measures outcomes without custody of user funds or private keys.

How is Ekko different from a notification provider?

Notification providers deliver messages. Ekko starts earlier and ends later: it detects wallet behaviour, matches intent, triggers the right wallet-scoped action, and measures whether the user returned, converted, or created value.
