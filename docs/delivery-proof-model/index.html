Delivery proof model

Ekko explains a missed opportunity through five fields so protocol teams can reason about what happened, who was affected, what message should have existed, and what impact silence created.

  1. Trigger

    What happened?

    The onchain or product state that should create a user or operator message.

  2. Affected cohort

    Who needed to know?

    Wallets, accounts, users, cohorts, and operators connected to the event.

  3. Expected notification

    What message should exist?

    The governed message, channel, pacing, suppression, approval, and provenance context.

  4. Counterfactual action

    What would action have changed?

    The user, support, revenue, retention, trust, or security outcome the team wanted to influence.

  5. Impact

    What did silence cost?

    The operational or commercial risk created when the message was not sent, suppressed incorrectly, or routed too late.
