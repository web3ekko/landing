Democratizing blockchain intelligence through natural language.

> SYSTEM_INTRODUCTION

Ekko is revolutionizing the Web3 experience with an innovative events oracle. Designed as a co-pilot for blockchain users, we stream process onchain and offchain events to create automated alerts and workflows without requiring any coding knowledge.

What started as "Why can't I just tell the computer what I want to monitor?" has evolved into a comprehensive platform serving traders and enterprise teams alike.

To empower Web3 users with seamless, automated solutions. We simplify complex tasks and provide real-time insights for smarter decision making.

We believe blockchain monitoring shouldn't require a CS degree. Natural language + AI = Accessible Intelligence.
