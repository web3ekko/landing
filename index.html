Frequently asked questions

Questions before you build.

Clear answers about agentic notification infrastructure, team controls, and what is available today.

What is Ekko?+

Ekko helps protocols and individuals operate plans that keep people informed when relevant wallet or protocol activity occurs.

Does every triggered message need manual approval?+

No. After a protocol activates its plan and message rules, qualifying alerts can automatically generate and send messages under those rules.

Does Ekko connect to or control user wallets?+

No. Ekko can use approved wallet context supplied by a provider, but it does not replace the wallet connector, request custody, or control funds.

What can I evaluate today?+

WalletAlert, natural-language alert creation, API, Engagement, Protocol Ops, and supported Marketplace alert patterns are available. Heartbeat and Agents are Coming Soon.

How do individual plans work?+

Individual plans are a first-class part of Ekko and help people stay current on activity that matters to them. Detailed enrollment, packaging, and pricing are still being defined.
